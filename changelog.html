Installing on macOS? One command clears the Gatekeeper warning

macOS builds are not signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate yet, so the first launch can be blocked with “MPE is damaged and can’t be opened” or “unverified developer”. Clear the quarantine flag once — afterwards the app opens normally, forever:

$ xattr -cr /Applications/MPE.app

Prefer Finder? Control-click MPE in Applications → Open → Open. Full walkthrough in the macOS first-run guide.
